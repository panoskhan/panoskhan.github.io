How to use the AI SEO Brief Generator

When to use it

Use this generator before writing a new page or article, when you want a writer, a colleague or yourself to start from a clear plan rather than a blank page. A brief keeps content focused on one search intent and one audience.

Step by step

  1. Enter the main keyword the page should rank for.
  2. Describe the audience as specifically as you can.
  3. Add the business type and the country or city you target.
  4. Generate the brief and adapt the headings and questions to your own expertise.

Example

A physiotherapy clinic in Athens targeting "sports injury rehabilitation" receives a brief with search intent, a suggested title, section headings and questions patients commonly ask.

Good to know

Treat the brief as a starting structure. Add first-hand experience, local details and real examples, because those are what make a page worth ranking.
