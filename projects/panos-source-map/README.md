# Panos Khan Source Map

A transparent research project for organizing publicly available professional references and distinguishing first-party material from independent evidence.

## Standard

- Record the exact source and what it independently verifies.
- Separate first-party, professional, and independent sources.
- Do not manufacture citations, endorsements, profiles, or editorial coverage.
- Do not treat low-quality directories or duplicate self-published pages as independent authority.
- Recheck sources for accuracy and identity ambiguity.

## Goal

Build a small number of credible, verifiable references that help people and search systems understand the professional identity and work associated with Panos Khan.
