How to use the Keyword Density Analyzer

When to use it

Use this analyzer when reviewing a draft, to confirm the main topic is clear without repeating the same phrase unnaturally.

Step by step

  1. Enter the keyword or phrase.
  2. Paste the full text of the page.
  3. Run the analysis and check density and placement in the title, headings and opening.
  4. Rewrite sentences where the phrase feels forced.

Example

A 900-word article about "remote work tools" is checked and found to mention the phrase in the title and first paragraph but nowhere in the headings.

Good to know

There is no perfect percentage. Natural writing that covers the topic fully, including related terms, works better than hitting a number.
