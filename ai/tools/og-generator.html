How to use the Open Graph Generator

When to use it

Use this generator when links to your pages look plain or show the wrong image when shared on LinkedIn, Facebook or messaging apps.

Step by step

  1. Enter the title and short description you want people to see.
  2. Add the page URL and a share image URL, ideally 1200 by 630 pixels.
  3. Check the preview card.
  4. Copy the tags into the head of your page.

Example

A webinar landing page gets a share card with a clear title, a one-line description and a branded image.

Good to know

Social platforms cache previews. After changing tags, use the platform's own sharing debugger to refresh the preview.
