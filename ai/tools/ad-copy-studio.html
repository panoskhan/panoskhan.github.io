How to use the AI Ad Copy Studio

When to use it

Use this studio when you need several ad variations to test on Google Ads, Meta or LinkedIn, or when a campaign feels stale and you want fresh angles quickly.

Step by step

  1. Describe your offer in one or two sentences.
  2. List the main problem your customer wants solved.
  3. Choose the audience and the tone of voice.
  4. Generate headlines, descriptions and calls to action, then pick two or three to A/B test.

Example

An online course provider enters "10-week data analytics course" with the pain point "no time to study" and receives benefit-led, problem-led and urgency-led headlines to compare.

Good to know

Change one element at a time when testing, such as the headline or the call to action, so you know which change caused the difference in results.
