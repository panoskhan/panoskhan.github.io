How to use the AI Decision Risk Simulator

When to use it

Use this simulator before launching an AI project that touches customers, personal data or important decisions, so risks are discussed before rather than after launch.

Step by step

  1. Describe the planned AI use case in plain language.
  2. Select how sensitive the data involved is.
  3. Run the simulation and read the risk levels for legal, bias, security, adoption and brand.
  4. Turn the recommended human review steps into a checklist for your project.

Example

A recruitment agency planning to rank CVs automatically sees elevated bias and legal risk and is advised to keep a human reviewer for every rejection.

Good to know

The scores are a structured conversation starter, not legal advice. For regulated uses, confirm obligations under the EU AI Act and GDPR with a qualified professional.
