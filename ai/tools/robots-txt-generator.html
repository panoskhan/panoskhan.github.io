How to use the Robots.txt Generator

When to use it

Use this generator when launching a website, moving to a new platform, or when admin or test areas should not be crawled by search engines.

Step by step

  1. Enter the full URL of your sitemap.
  2. List any folders that should not be crawled, one per line.
  3. Choose which crawlers the rules apply to.
  4. Upload the generated file as robots.txt to the root of your domain.

Example

A shop blocks /cart/ and /checkout/ from crawling and points crawlers to https://example.com/sitemap.xml.

Good to know

Robots.txt controls crawling, not indexing. To keep a page out of search results, use a noindex tag on the page instead of blocking it.
