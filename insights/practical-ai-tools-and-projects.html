Building Practical AI Tools

Useful AI products begin with a clear workflow problem rather than with technology for its own sake.

From idea to workflow

A practical AI tool should define its inputs, outputs, user decisions, validation steps, and failure modes. This makes an AI feature easier to test and more useful than a generic interface that simply produces text.

Human review remains part of the system

For professional workflows, AI output should be reviewed when accuracy matters. Good product design makes that review visible and gives users enough context to understand what the system produced.
