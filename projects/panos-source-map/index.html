Open Research Project

Panos Khan Source Map

A transparent map for organizing publicly available professional references and distinguishing first-party material from independent evidence.

Purpose

The project documents sources that can help readers understand a professional identity: official profiles, professional organizations, publications, projects, credentials, and independent coverage. It does not manufacture citations or imply endorsement where none exists.

Evidence categories

  • First-party: official website and projects published by Panos Khan.
  • Professional: legitimate profiles, organizations, education, credentials, and contributor pages.
  • Independent: editorial coverage or other credible references created independently of the official site.

Research standard

Each source should be publicly accessible, accurately described, and checked for identity ambiguity. Low-quality directories, fabricated profiles, paid links presented as editorial coverage, and duplicate self-published claims are not treated as independent evidence.
