What Website Health checks

Four dimensions are scored. Each maps to a measurable business outcome.

Messaging clarity

Does your homepage communicate what you do, who it's for, and why it matters — in seconds?

Discoverability

Can search engines and AI systems find, index, and understand your pages correctly?

Trust & credibility

Do visitors find the signals they need to trust your organisation before converting?

Conversion readiness

Are your calls to action, forms, and conversion paths clear, accessible, and frictionless?

How recommendations work

Prioritised by impact

Recommendations are ranked by the Recommendation Engine using effort, impact, and urgency — so you always know what to fix first.

Actionable, not generic

Each recommendation links to the specific dimension it improves, with an estimated time to resolve and clear success criteria.

Backed by the Knowledge Graph

The Knowledge Engine surfaces related guides, tools, and downloads that are relevant to your specific findings.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a live website scanner?

No. Website Health is a structured self-assessment tool. You describe your website's context and the Health Engine evaluates the information you provide. No live crawling or network requests are made to your website.

How is the health score calculated?

The Health Engine scores four dimensions — messaging clarity, discoverability, trust & credibility, and conversion readiness — and combines them into an overall health score. Each dimension contributes equally to the final score.

Where is my data stored?

All processing happens entirely in your browser. No data is sent to a server. Your input is used only to generate the report and is discarded when you close the page.

What happened to the Website Audit tool?

Website Audit has been promoted and expanded into Website Health — the first full pillar of Digital Health. All legacy URLs at /ai/tools/website-audit.html and /tools/website-audit.html redirect here automatically. Your bookmarks and links continue to work.

Can I download or share the report?

Yes. After generating a report, the Report Engine provides Download and Share actions. Use these to save the report or share a summary with your team.

How does Website Health relate to Platform Intelligence?

Website Health is the first fully implemented pillar of Digital Health, which is the flagship product powered by Platform Intelligence. It reuses all shared engines — Health, Recommendation, Knowledge, Search, and Report — without duplicating any functionality.
