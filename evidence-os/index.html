Evidence OS

A public evidence layer for research, projects and claims. Evidence OS is designed to make important statements traceable: from the original claim to its sources, verification status and confidence.

← Back to Panos Khan
Claim→ Source→ Evidence→ Verification→ Confidence→ History

Claims

Record exactly what is being asserted, with context and timestamps.

Sources

Attach first-party, independent, academic, editorial or technical sources.

Evidence

Keep the supporting material and explain why it supports the claim.

Verification

Track whether a claim is unverified, supported, independently corroborated or disputed.

Confidence

Separate evidence quality from certainty instead of presenting guesses as facts.

Provenance

Preserve when evidence was added, changed or superseded.

Prototype foundation · Publicly documented methodology · No claim is considered verified merely because it appears in this system.
