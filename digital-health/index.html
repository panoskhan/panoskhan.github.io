How Digital Health works

Every health check runs through the same shared platform runtime — no custom pipelines, no one-off tools.

Step 1
Describe your context

Tell us about your website, device, project, or AI workflow and what you want to improve.

Step 2
Health Engine evaluates

The shared Health Engine scores dimensions, identifies issues, and surfaces patterns.

Step 3
Report Engine renders

A canonical health report is generated with your score, issues, and a prioritised action plan.

Step 4
Act on recommendations

Follow the recommended action plan and use the linked resources to make lasting improvements.
