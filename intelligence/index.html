FAQ

Is Platform Intelligence a medical or clinical health tool?

No. Platform Intelligence is about digital systems — websites, devices, software projects, and AI workflows. It has nothing to do with medical or personal health.

Does Platform Intelligence send my data to a server?

No. All health checks run entirely in your browser. No form data is sent to any Panos Khan server. Your inputs stay on your device.

What does "Panos Khan Verified" mean?

When a health check scores 90/100 or higher, the report earns a Panos Khan Verified badge. This is a quality indicator, not an official certification — it signals that the subject meets high professional standards as measured by the platform.

How do I add a new health check tool?

Register the capability in platform-registry.json, add its relationships to knowledge-graph.json, connect it to the shared engines, and keep the canonical path under /intelligence/. See the product hub guide for full instructions.

Which health pillars are live?

Website Health is now live at /intelligence/website-health/. Device Health links to the live Device Service. Project Health and AI Workflow Health are planned for future milestones.
