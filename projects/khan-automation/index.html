KHAN Automation

A content pipeline where AI does the heavy lifting and a human always makes the final call. It plans, drafts, checks and packages YouTube content, and nothing goes live without my approval.

Why I built it

Most "AI content machines" pump out unchecked videos with copied scripts, missing sources and hidden affiliate links. I wanted the opposite: a system that is fast, but refuses to let weak or dishonest content through. Every claim needs a source, every affiliate link needs a disclosure, and every publish needs a human yes.

Eight agents, one pipeline

Orchestrator

Moves each topic through the pipeline, one step at a time.

Research Scout

Collects topic ideas for review.

Topic Scorer

Scores demand, audience fit and originality. Weak topics are rejected.

Writer

Produces a structured brief and script with claims and sources.

QA Gate

Blocks copied scripts, unsupported income claims, missing sources, missing disclosures and unfinished placeholders.

Publisher

Builds an upload pack after approval. Test content can never go live.

Watchdog

Restarts stalled tasks and freezes an agent if errors spike.

Budget Controller

Daily and monthly spending caps. Default budget is €0.

How a video moves through it

  1. A topic is scored. Only strong topics continue.
  2. The system writes a brief. I add real test results from my own work.
  3. The QA Gate checks the script. If it fails, a revision brief explains exactly why.
  4. I approve it. Without that approval, nothing moves.
  5. An upload pack is created. I upload and record the link.

Safety built in

  • Human approval gate before any publishing.
  • Global kill switch that stops every agent instantly.
  • Zero-cost default: paid AI calls are blocked unless a budget is set.
  • Weekly volume cap so quality never turns into spam.
  • Automated test suite: 26 tests covering the pipeline, QA rules and safety controls, all passing.

Status and roadmap

Wave 1 is working end to end in manual mode: scoring, briefs, QA, approval, upload packs and publish tracking.

Next steps:

  • Direct AI model connection (today briefs are completed by hand)
  • Automatic web research for topics
  • Live YouTube upload
  • Scheduling for continuous operation
  • Wave 2 agents for tool testing and media production
