How tools work

Tools use deterministic client-side logic from your inputs. They do not scrape live sites or send form data to a Panos Khan server.

Privacy defaults

No privileged installs. No account required. Optional recent-tool ids may be stored in localStorage for a future Workspace sync design.

Limits

Outputs are decision aids, not legal, medical, or security certifications. Validate before production use.

FAQ

Do these tools call external AI APIs with my inputs?

Current tools use deterministic client-side logic from your inputs. They do not scrape live sites or send form data to a Panos Khan server.

Can I use the outputs in client work?

Yes as decision aids. Validate recommendations before production use. Outputs are not legal, medical, or security certifications.

Where is long-form documentation?

Product-level notes live on this hub. Cross-product architecture and templates live in Panos Khan Docs.

What about multi-step AI agents?

Agents are intentionally deferred until the core catalog, docs, and capability map are mature. See the Agents section below.
