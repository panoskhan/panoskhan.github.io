Methodology

Evidence OS separates identity, evidence quality and confidence. A name match alone never establishes identity.

1 · Claim

State exactly what is being asserted, including context and date where relevant.

2 · Source

Prefer primary sources, then independent reputable sources. Record the source link and role.

3 · Evidence

Explain what the source actually supports. Do not turn proximity or repetition into proof.

4 · Verification

Use explicit states: Verified, Supported, Unverified or Disputed. Keep uncertainty visible.

5 · Confidence

Confidence is an assessment of the evidence available, not a guarantee of truth.

6 · Provenance

Preserve the basis and update date so later reviewers can understand how a record was formed.
