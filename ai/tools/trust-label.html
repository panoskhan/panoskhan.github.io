How to use the AI Trust Label Generator

When to use it

Use this generator when you use AI in your services or content and want to explain it openly to clients and website visitors.

Step by step

  1. Enter your business name.
  2. Describe where and how AI is used in your work.
  3. Generate the five-part statement.
  4. Edit it so every sentence is accurate, then publish it on your website or in proposals.

Example

A marketing agency that uses AI for first drafts publishes a statement explaining that all client content is reviewed and edited by a person before delivery.

Good to know

Only publish commitments you actually follow. A transparency statement builds trust when it matches real practice.
