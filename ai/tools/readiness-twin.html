How to use the AI Readiness Twin

When to use it

Use this planner when your organization wants to start with AI but is unsure where to begin, or when leadership asks for both quick results and a longer-term plan.

Step by step

  1. Select your industry.
  2. Describe the biggest operational challenge you face today.
  3. Generate the plan and review the 30-day quick wins first.
  4. Use the 12-month track to discuss budget, governance and training with your team.

Example

A small logistics company whose main challenge is manual order entry receives quick wins such as document extraction pilots and a 12-month track covering data quality and staff training.

Good to know

Start with one quick win that has a clear owner and a measurable result. Early success makes the longer programme much easier to approve.
