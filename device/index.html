FAQ

Do you distribute repair binaries?

No. Device Service is guidance-first. Safe assets live in Downloads and are documented HTML/PDF/template style resources.

Can you help unlock a device I do not own?

No. Support assumes lawful ownership or authorized service. Activation lock and ownership bypass topics are out of scope.

What should I do first when a device fails?

Confirm power and data backup status, capture exact symptoms, then follow the platform guidance or contact a qualified technician.
